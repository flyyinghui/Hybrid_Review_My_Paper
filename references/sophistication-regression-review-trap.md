# Sophistication-Regression Trap in Multi-Round Reviews (2026-07-24)

## Pattern
When a paper section goes through multiple review rounds (vN → vN+1 → vN+2),
higher mathematical sophistication in the N+2 version can mask a LOGICAL REGRESSION
compared to N+1.

## Case Study: λ_KLS Derivation (v6→v7→v8)

| Version | Math Level | Score | Verdict |
|:--|:--|:--|:--|
| v6 | Moderate | 3.7/10 | Overclaims, hidden assumptions |
| v7 | Moderate | 6.3/10 | Honest, transparent, close to acceptable |
| v8 | **High** (Einstein metrics, Casimir spectra, branching rules) | 4.0/10 | REGRESSION — circular, decorative, incompatible |

## Why v8 Scored Lower

1. **Circular postulate**: Einstein constant c = -1/6 was chosen BACKWARD from desired answer 35/3. No independent physical motivation.

2. **Decorative mathematics**: Part II (SU(6) representation theory, SO(3) branching) spanned 120 lines but contributed zero independent logical force — three computational paths all failed, fell back to Part I's result.

3. **Incompatibility**: v8 contradicted V57's existing axiom system (P29 metric normalization, A_DIM3 dimensional reduction), making it impossible to integrate.

4. **Self-correction exposed circularity**: The derivation openly corrected the postulate from -1/3 to -1/6 mid-stream, proving the value wasn't derived from principles.

## Review Protocol for Sophistication-Regression Detection

When reviewing a vN+2 that uses heavier mathematical machinery than vN+1:

1. **Verify the key postulate is not chosen backward**: Does it have independent motivation, or is its value uniquely determined to yield the target number?

2. **Check if new sections are load-bearing or decorative**: Does the advanced mathematics independently derive the result, or does it compute → fail → fall back?

3. **Audit compatibility with existing axioms**: Does the new framework align with or contradict the paper's established mathematical structure?

4. **Compare transparency**: Is the new version more or less honest about what's derived vs assumed?

## Meta-Lesson
"Transparent ad-hoc division (v7's |ρ|²/3) is better than dressed-up circularity (v8's 'Einstein metric postulate'). In peer review, honesty beats sophistication when the sophistication is decorative."
